How it works

1. Who donates?

Restaurants, hotels, canteens, hostels, events, supermarkets and households list surplus edible food.

2. Who receives it?

Verified NGOs, shelters and community groups that need food nearby.

3. How is it matched?

The AI Matcher scores receivers on distance, quantity fit, deadline and food type.

4. Who picks it up?

Nearby volunteers accept the pickup and follow an optimized route.

5. Where does it go?

Straight to the matched receiver, confirmed with digital proof of delivery.

6. What impact?

Meals, kg saved, people served and CO₂ avoided are tracked live.

Trust & safety: organizations and volunteers are verified by admins, every pickup is tracked. The platform does not itself certify food as safe for consumption; donors must follow local food-safety rules.
